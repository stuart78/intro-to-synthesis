Intro to Synthesis — a free hands-on workshop at Synth Library Portland.

March 29 at 3 PM.

No experience needed. We'll start from zero — what is voltage, what is a waveform, what makes one sound different from another — and by the end you'll have built a full synth voice from scratch in VCV Rack.

Three questions drive everything:
What does it sound like? (Timbre)
What note? (Pitch)
When does it happen? (Rhythm)

We'll reconstruct the Minimoog signal path — the architecture behind most synthesizers ever made — then take it apart and see what happens when you remove the rules.

Bring a laptop with VCV Rack 2 installed (free, link in bio). Headphones help but aren't required.

See you there.
